<b>Just between us.</b> Nothing and no one in between.
Chat, Calls, Groups, Shared spaces and tools.

Jami keeps your conversations between you and the people you choose. No server reads them, no company keeps them, not even ours. You don't give a phone number or an email to start: your identity is created on your phone and belongs to you alone. Messages, calls and files travel directly between devices, encrypted end to end. No ads, no stolen data, no algorithm, totally free.

<b>Between friends</b>
Chat one-to-one or in groups. Make audio and video calls, send voice and video messages, photos and files with no size limit, and share your location. Only the contacts you accept see your name and photo.

<b>Between colleagues</b>
Hold group video calls, share your screen, record a call, and send large files. Jami works over the Internet and on a local network, at the office or anywhere else.

<b>Between citizens</b>
Jami doesn't depend on any company. It can work on a local network without Internet access, and its code is open for anyone to check.

<b>Your identity, on all your devices</b>
Create your identity in seconds: a photo, a display name and a username are all optional. Add it to your other phone, tablet or computer, or restore it from a backup. Jami is available on Android, Android TV, iOS, GNU/Linux, Windows and macOS.

<b>For organizations</b>
With JAMS (Jami Account Management Server), organizations let their members sign in with their usual credentials and manage their own Jami community, while keeping Jami's distributed architecture.

<b>For developers and power users</b>
Jami can also connect a phone line as a SIP client. Extensions add audio filters, auto answer, background replacement, watermark and live transcription. Its portable library can be reused in your own projects, including IoT.
